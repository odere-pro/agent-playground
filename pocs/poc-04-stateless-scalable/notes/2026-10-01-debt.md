## Load and measurement

- **CPU per call swings up to 2x between identical runs** on this Docker Desktop VM, whatever the model capacity. Candidates are vCPU placement on efficiency cores or CFS quota throttling at `cpus: 1.0`; neither is measured. Written: `notes/2026-10-01-load-results.md`, "Shared bottleneck check". Owner: 122 X-5 (a run on Linux or kind with pinned CPUs).
- **4 pairs do not fit the 7.9 GiB VM.** In every 4-pair run, 6 to 13% of the chassis and workload samples are above the 1.0-vCPU cap. Written: the load note, "Quiet pass: throughput". Owner: 122 X-5.
- **The sidecar hop is not isolated.** Sidecar minus inprocess is 1.0 ms at p50 and 0.0 ms at p95, below Locust's 1 ms resolution, and it also counts the difference between two `echo_python` hosts. A direct figure needs a chassis-side timer around the outbound A2A call, per request; the chassis has none. Written: the load note, "the sidecar hop". Owner: 122 X-5, with PoC-7 (`observability-expert`) for the timer.
- **The idempotency cost at saturation comes from 2 alternations only** (about 3% of throughput, about 1 ms of chassis CPU per call; the ranges touch). Written: the load note, "Idempotency cost, measured back to back". Owner: 122 X-5.

## Idempotency and Valkey

- **The store key is scoped per agent, not per caller.** A caller who knows another caller's key can hold it, learn it exists, or get the cached answer. When auth arrives, the store key and the fingerprint must include the principal. Written: SECURITY.md section 7; review. Owner: PoC-8, 018 H-18, 022 H-6.
- **Any caller can fill Valkey.** `--maxmemory 256mb`, `noeviction`, no per-caller quota; once full, every keyed call is 503. Written: SECURITY.md section 7. Owner: PoC-8, 022 H-6. suggested there: `ttl_s` 3600, `max_entry_bytes` 65536, and a memory alert.
- **Valkey sizing.** About 1.5 KB per call at `ttl_s` 86400 gives only about 2 calls per second steady with 256 MB; the kind drills filled it in 11 to 60 minutes. Size Valkey from rate × TTL × about 1.5 KB, or shorten `ttl_s`. Written: `notes/2026-10-01-container-roles.md`, "Valkey and the idempotency TTL". Owner: 024 CH-3, 018 H-18.
- **The 503 text misleads when the store is full.** `state_unavailable` says "the chassis cannot reach its state store; retry later" (`core/inbound.py`, `PUBLIC_MESSAGES`), but the store is reachable and full. Written: the container-roles note. Owner: suggested: 018 H-18 (`developer`).
- **The cache holds requests and answers in the clear,** for `ttl_s` with no ceiling, over `valkey://` with no TLS; the Valkey password is on `valkey-server`'s command line. Written: SECURITY.md section 7. Owner: 018 H-18 (TLS, a `ttl_s` ceiling, retention), 026 CH-4.
- **The Valkey client does not retry.** valkey-py's retries are 0, so a transient error is a 503 `state_unavailable` on a keyed call. Written: review. Owner: suggested: 018 H-18 (`developer`).
- **No test for a renew slower than the fence.** The fence at `0.8 * lease_s` and a renew that fails are tested (`packages/chassis/tests/test_idempotency.py::test_a_holder_cut_off_from_the_store_is_fenced_before_its_lease_ends`); a renew that succeeds but takes longer than the time left to the fence is not. Written: review. Owner: suggested: 018 H-18 (`tester`).
- **A replica on older code reads the too-large marker as unreadable.** During a rolling upgrade, a replica without the `too_large` state answers 503 `state_unavailable` for that key, a retryable error that keeps failing until `ttl_s`. Written: review. Owner: suggested: 018 H-18, with a rolling-upgrade rule in 024 CH-3.

## Events

- **Dapr fails two `EventPort` cases by design:** dead-letter events lack `deadletterreason` and `deadletterattempts`, and retries follow `resiliency.yaml`, not `max_attempts`; one consumer group per app id. Strict xfails in `packages/chassis/tests/integration/test_dapr_events_contract.py`. daprd also reads `/dapr/subscribe` once at start. Written: contract v3, "Known gaps"; `notes/2026-10-01-dapr-vs-broker.md`. Owner: ADR-004 (`chassis-architect`).
- **`DAPR_MAX_RETRIES` is set nowhere,** so the adapter's check of `max_attempts` against daprd's retries never runs in this stack (`adapters/dapr/events.py`). Written: the Dapr note, "What Dapr did not give for free". Owner: ADR-004; moot if Dapr is dropped.
- **Two daprd ports bind every interface with no auth:** the metrics port 9090 and the internal gRPC port (random, mTLS off). Written: SECURITY.md section 7; the Dapr note. Owner: `platform-security`, 026 CH-4, only if Dapr is kept.
- **`KafkaEvents`: a failed dead-letter publish ends that subscription's consumer task.** Written: contract v3, "Known gaps"; 019 H-17. Owner: 019 H-17 (`developer`).
- **The PoC Kafka is open.** PLAINTEXT with no auth, the controller listener on every interface, topics created automatically, a writable root. Written: SECURITY.md section 7. Owner: 020 X-8.
- **Result events carry the run's `input` and `output`** on that open broker. The key is published only as its sha256 hex. Written: SECURITY.md section 7. Owner: 020 X-8 (ACLs), 019 H-17 (`input` opt-in).
- **The vendored CloudEvents schema was transcribed from memory.** It must be diffed against upstream cloudevents/spec v1.0.2 before it counts as vendored (`packages/contract-suites/src/chassis_contracts/data/cloudevents-1.0.schema.json`). Written: contract v3, "Known gaps". Owner: `chassis-architect`.
- **Event-triggered runs are not built.** A non-null `spec.events.consume` is refused at load. Written: contract v3, "Known gaps". Owner: 019 H-17.
- **A broker other than Kafka** must pass the whole `EventPortContract` against a real broker with no xfail (ADR-004, item 8). Written: `notes/backlog-changes.md`, "Not applied". Owner: 060 H-23.

Native, OpenAI, and Anthropic now poll `is_disconnected` every 250 ms in complete mode, cancel the run, free its budget and its idempotency key, and answer an empty 499. Written: contract v3, "A client that leaves a complete call"; `packages/chassis/tests/test_client_disconnect.py`. MCP is still not watched (above).
- **Result events published the raw idempotency key.** They now carry its sha256 hex in `data` and in the `idempotencykey` and `partitionkey` extensions (`packages/chassis/tests/test_result_events.py`). SECURITY.md section 7 and contract v3 now agree, so the "one of them is out of date" item in `notes/backlog-changes.md` is resolved.
