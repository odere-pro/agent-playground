# 2026-10-29: the test fixture kit

Scope item: "a test fixture kit for business logic authors". Exit criterion it feeds: "a scaffolded agent's `make test` passes offline on its first commit". Package: `packages/agent-testkit`.

## How each process starts

- Workload: `python -m workload_a2a serve --handle <handle> --name <name> --uds <tmp>/workload.sock --drain-timeout-s 1 --log-level warning`, with the interpreter that runs pytest. `PYTHONPATH` starts with a guard folder whose `chassis` and `chassis_contracts` raise `ImportError`. `CHASSIS_MODEL_URL` and `CHASSIS_TOOL_URL` point at the chassis proxy port.
- Chassis: `<source> -I <kit>/data/chassis_serve.py --uds <tmp>/chassis.sock serve --config <tmp>/chassis.yaml --port <p> --proxy-port <q> --drain-delay-s 0 --drain-timeout-s 1 --startup-wait-s 30`. The config is `profile: fake`, `spec.engine.connector: sidecar`, `spec.engine.uds: <workload.sock>`. In the monorepo, `<source>` is `uv run --project <repo> --package chassis --isolated --no-dev --frozen --offline python`: a fresh env from `uv.lock` holding the chassis and its deps, no `workload_a2a`.
- The kit waits for `/.well-known/agent-card.json`, then `/ready`, over the sockets. It stops both with SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after 10 s.

## What is offline

- Every test of the kit runs under `scripts/check_offline.sh`. The test process opens Unix sockets only.
- The model is the chassis's `fake` adapter. No key reaches either process.
- The isolated chassis env installs from the lock with `--offline`; the wheels come from uv's cache.

## Evidence

`WorkloadContract` bound to echo-python (isolated chassis env) and echo-pydanticai (`@current-python`): 47 passed in about 40 s. A workload that imports `chassis` fails to start. A child pytest with only `pyproject.toml` and `pytest_plugins` gets the session fixtures.

## Not yet

- `chassis serve` has no `--uds` and no `--profile fake` without a config; see `2026-10-29-testkit-asks.md`.
- The `uv tool run --from <spec>` form resolves, so it needs a warm cache. Its versions are not pinned to the platform lock.
- The cases are the A2A lane only. The in-memory lane (`inprocess`) needs the chassis in the test process, which the kit refuses.
- Not run yet on a repo scaffolded by `agentctl`.
